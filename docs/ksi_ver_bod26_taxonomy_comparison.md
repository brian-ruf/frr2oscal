# KSI Taxonomy, VDR/VER Rules, and BOD 26-04: Three-Way Element Comparison

**Purpose.** This table aligns the formal KSI taxonomy with the FedRAMP VDR/VER rule set (version 2026.10.08.01) and CISA BOD 26-04 (June 10, 2026), including the BOD 26-04 Standardized Data Schema (CISA, 2026). Rows are grouped by the KSI taxonomy element; where VER or BOD introduces a concept with no KSI counterpart, the KSI cell is blank.

**Sources.**
- **[KSI taxonomy](https://patterns.rufrisk.com/books/key-security-indicators-ksis/page/taxonomy)**  as it exists Oct 29, 2026
- **[FedRAMP-Published Consolidated Rules 26 JSON File](https://github.com/FedRAMP/rules/blob/a89d78477ddea98dc57eca24f5007794c2b4eeb6/schemas/fedramp-consolidated-rules.schema.json):** version 2026.10.08.01
- **[CISA BOD 26-04](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/directives/bod-26-04-prioritizing-security-updates-based-risk)**
- **[CISA BOD 26-04 Standardized Data Schema XLSX](https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/bod-26-04-standardized-data-schema.xlsx)**`

**Schema field notation.** BOD schema fields are prefixed by their index (`cdm_vuln.*`, `cdm_hwam.*`, `cdm_cyhy_vuln.*`). `agency_provided=1` means the agency submits the value; `agency_provided=0` means CISA or a pipeline derives it.

## Cross-Framework Notes

1. **The provider-to-agency-to-CISA chain.** VER rules govern the provider-to-FedRAMP/agency leg. BOD governs the agency-to-CISA leg. The two rules are complementary but neither defines the boundary or the per-asset translation (e.g., mapping a provider's PAIN rating to a BOD timeline input for CDM submission). That gap falls on the agency.

2. **PAIN vs. Table 1 inputs are not equivalent.** PAIN rates the impact on agencies (N0–N5), while BOD's Technical Impact rates adversary control over the asset (partial/total). A provider-estimated PAIN rating cannot be converted to BOD Technical Impact without additional information. Agencies consuming VER reports cannot mechanically derive a BOD compliance status without independently determining Technical Impact and KEV status.

3. **Timing offsets.** VER's `VDR-TFR-PVR` clock starts at evaluation (with up to 5–14 days to evaluate after detection). BOD's clock starts at the earlier of KEV addition or CDM identification. For an N5, internet-reachable, likely exploitable vulnerability at Class C, the two clocks can produce materially different deadlines from the same detection event.

4. **KSI taxonomy as an abstraction layer.** The KSI taxonomy was designed for provider-defined KSI measurements (capabilities, processes, resources). It maps onto VER concepts through analogies—PAIN maps to a degree-of-health scale, V&V cadences map to Cycle—but VER is operationally much more specific. The taxonomy maps less cleanly onto BOD because BOD's logic is algorithmic rather than measurement-chain-based.
